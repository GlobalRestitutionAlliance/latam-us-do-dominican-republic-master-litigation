## 1. Executive Mission & Jurisprudential Scope

The **United States – Dominican Republic Master Restitution Litigation Framework** constitutes the institutional, unassailable legal infrastructure established by the **Global Restitution Alliance** to enforce final, binding international judgments and liquidate valid historical claims arising from 1916-2026 of unlawful United States intervention in Dominican Republic (1916-2026).

Anchored by the landmark 1916-1924 Occupation, 1965 Intervention, this repository operationalizes sovereign asset garnishment, commercial attachment, and exequatur enforcement across municipal and foreign jurisdictions.

### Core Jurisprudential Pillars

1. **Direct Enforcement of Final Judgment (*Res Judicata*):** Full recognition and domestic conversion of the binding judgment holding the United States in breach of customary international law obligations and mandating full reparation under the *Chorzów Factory* standard (PCIJ Ser. A, No. 17).
2. **Sovereign Immunity Forfeiture:** Documented bad-faith default triggering statutory exceptions to sovereign immunity under the Foreign Sovereign Immunities Act (FSIA, 28 U.S.C. §§ 1605(a)(3), 1610(a)(6)) and the UN Convention on Jurisdictional Immunities of States (UNCSI).
3. **Historical Continuum & Concession Nullity:** Substantive recovery spanning 1916-2026 of unlawful intervention, occupation, and economic spoliation.
4. **Inoculated Settlement Architecture:** All recovered proceeds and commercial asset liquidations are channeled into an independent Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust (26 U.S.C. § 468B) dedicated to humanitarian recovery, infrastructure revitalization, and victim restitution.
